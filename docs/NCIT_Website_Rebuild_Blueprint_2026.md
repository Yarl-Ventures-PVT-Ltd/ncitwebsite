## **The new NCIT website must behave like a digital chamber, not a brochure** 

###### **02 / EXECUTIVE DIRECTION** 

A stakeholder should understand NCIT, discover the Northern technology ecosystem, find members, see opportunities, engage with NCIT, join the Chamber, and manage membership without needing to contact staff for basic tasks. 

###### **Recommended positioning** 

### **Northern Sri Lanka’s Gateway to Technology, Talent & Global Opportunity.** 

###### **From the North to the World.** 

##### **Strategic outcomes** 

- Position NCIT as the trusted, unified and leading identity for the Northern technology industry. 

- Create one credible entry point for technology companies, professionals, training institutions, startups, freelancers, students, investors, government institutions and development partners. 

- Make the five-district Northern technology ecosystem visible, searchable and investable. 

- Turn membership into a measurable digital lifecycle: apply -> verify -> approve -> pay -> activate -> engage -> renew. 

- Create a current public record of NCIT leadership, governance, projects, events, policy work and impact while retaining historical archives. 

- Give the Secretariat and Board a manageable CMS and member administration system with clear ownership and auditability.

## **A modern Northern technology identity without becoming a generic “cyber” website** 

###### **04 / BRAND & POSITIONING** 

The site should feel like a serious international chamber and investment gateway: clean, bright, precise, credible and human - with technology expressed through data, network and interface language rather than excessive neon effects. 

##### **Recommended website-level vision (Board approval)** 

To position Northern Sri Lanka as a globally connected, trusted and competitive technology region - creating opportunity, investment and digital prosperity from the North to the world. 

##### **Recommended website-level mission (Board approval)** 

NCIT unifies and represents the Northern technology ecosystem, strengthens industry standards and talent, connects members to markets and investment, advocates for enabling policy, and builds partnerships that accelerate sustainable technology-led growth. 

##### **Brand pillars** 

- UNITY - one credible identity for the Northern technology ecosystem. 

- TRUST - transparent governance, verified membership and professional standards. 

- OPPORTUNITY - business, careers, investment, partnerships and market access. 

- GLOBAL CONNECTIVITY - North-to-world relationships, diaspora, national and international links. 

- INNOVATION - startups, digital transformation, emerging technology and problem solving. 

- REGIONAL IMPACT - meaningful growth across all five Northern districts. 

##### **Tone of voice** 

- Confident, contemporary, inclusive and precise. 

- Plain international English; no inflated claims without evidence. 

- Use action verbs: connect, build, accelerate, invest, collaborate, discover, join. 

- Avoid outdated phrases such as “information highway”, generic “best in world” claims, and long institutional paragraphs. 

- Show the North as a technology and opportunity region, not only as a tourism/location story.

## **Components that must stay consistent across every device** 

###### **08 / GLOBAL UX COMPONENTS** 

The design system should be implemented as reusable components in code and mirrored in the CMS where content editors need them. 

- Sticky desktop header; compact mobile header; utility actions for language, search, join and login. 

- Hero module: eyebrow, H1, 1-2 sentence value proposition, two CTAs, optional trust badge/stat. 

- Section header module: label, headline, concise body, optional action. 

- Member/company card; event card; article card; project card; opportunity/job card; report/resource card. 

- Data/stat module with source/date field to prevent unsupported or stale statistics. 

- Logo strip with accessible labels and grayscale-to-color hover optional. 

- Filter/sort/search system for directories and archives with mobile filter drawer. 

- CTA band with strong single purpose; avoid more than two equal-priority buttons. 

- Accordion for FAQs; tabs only when content remains accessible and URL-addressable where useful. 

- Toast/status pattern for portal actions; validation messages adjacent to fields; skeleton states for data loading. 

- Empty states that explain what happens next instead of blank screens. 

- Footer with About, key links, membership, legal, contact, newsletter and social; never overcrowd with historical navigation.

1. Visitor chooses membership type or uses the guided selector. 

2. Applicant creates/verifies an account and saves a draft. 

3. Applicant completes data and uploads required supporting documents. 

4. Applicant acknowledges governing rules and privacy notice, then submits. 

5. Membership Admin checks eligibility, documents and duplicates; requests more information if necessary. 

6. Authorized approver records decision. Rejections use standardized reason codes + optional internal note; sensitive internal comments never exposed publicly. 

7. Approved applicant receives invoice/payment request. Bank transfer can be supported alongside an approved online payment gateway. 

8. Finance verifies or gateway confirms payment; system issues receipt. 

9. Membership becomes Active; system creates member ID, public profile (if consent/policy), certificate and verified badge. 

10. Renewal reminders start before expiry; member renews and updates documents if required; lapsed state applies according to policy. 

###### **RECOMMENDED STATUS MODEL** 

Draft -> Submitted -> Under Review -> Additional Information Required -> Approved / Payment Due -> Active -> Renewal Due -> Expired / Suspended / Resigned. Rejected and Cancelled are terminal states with audit history.

## **Treat the member portal as a business system** 

###### **42 / SECURITY & PRIVACY** 

The new site will process applicant identities, registration documents, contact information, payments and membership records. Security and privacy controls are therefore release-blocking requirements. 

##### **Privacy / Sri Lanka PDPA** 

- Publish a clear privacy notice stating purposes, categories of data, legal basis/grounds, sharing/processors, retention, rights and contact route. 

- Map personal data by module: applicant/member data, identity/registration documents, event registrations, inquiries, analytics and communications. 

- Collect the minimum data required for each purpose; separate optional public-directory consent from mandatory membership processing. 

- Define retention: unsuccessful applications, expired members, identity documents, payment records, support tickets and logs should each have a policy. 

- Provide an operational process for access/correction/deletion/objection requests where applicable, with identity verification. 

- Use processor agreements and access controls for hosting, email, payment, analytics and storage providers. 

- Prepare breach/incident response and record access to sensitive member records. 

##### **Application security** 

- Role-based access control with least privilege; admin MFA mandatory. 

- Password hashing via Argon2id (or equally appropriate framework-supported modern algorithm); never plaintext or reversible encryption. 

- Rate limiting, bot protection, CSRF protections where relevant, output encoding and validated file uploads. 

- Private object storage for membership documents using short-lived signed URLs; malware scanning; file type/size restrictions. 

- Secure, HttpOnly, SameSite cookies; short privileged-session timeouts; session invalidation on password reset/security changes. 

- Comprehensive audit log for approvals, status changes, payments, role changes, document access and profile moderation. 

- Encrypted transport (TLS), HSTS, security headers/CSP, dependency scanning and patching. 

- Database backups encrypted and tested for restoration; production data never copied casually into developer/test environments. 

- Separate staging and production; secrets in secret manager/environment vault, never source control. 

- Quarterly access review and at least annual independent security review/pentest for the portal or after major changes.
