There was workshop held on 12.09.2016 in jaffna TCT Hall to educate students about social media those who completed the A/L examination.It’s about the beneficial side of social media and the ways to define career path towards #SocialMedia The workshop is organized by HigherStudy.lk in association with Birdfather. Northern Chamber of Information Technology supported as Community partner. There was around 150 Students was participated.
