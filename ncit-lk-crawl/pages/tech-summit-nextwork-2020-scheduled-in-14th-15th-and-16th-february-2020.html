The “Northern Chamber of Information Technology” (NCIT) together with the “Rotary Club of Jaffna” will be holding a International Tech Summit (NExTWORK 2020) in the Northern City of Jaffna. ICTA going to be the National Partner for the Summit .

NCIT and Rotary Club Jaffna both have an objective to build the Northern economy. As international access to the North opened up, with the new regional airport at Palaly and the plans for a ferry services from KKS Harbor, we need to prepare for greater international commercial partnerships. In addition the Sri Lankan Government has plans to build incubation centers in the North to create launch pads for Northern tech startups. As part of this preparation, we will be holding a Summit in 14th, 15th and 16th February 2020 at Tilko City Hotel, Jaffna. This will be the first of a regular annual series of Summits to be held in the Northern Province of Srilanka.

At this Summit, there will be a trade fair where technology companies from all over the world specially  India and Sri Lanka will meet and mix to share opportunities, technologies, and ideas. There will be a series of Tech Talks where industry experts will provide their insights to entrepreneurs, students, and potential clients.

The objective of this year Summit is to provide a two way window for Indian and Sri Lankan companies to collaborate to win wider markets nationally and internationally.

We are planning to setup the 40  stalls for “Tech Solution” Companies . Maximum to Indian Companies 20 ,Maximum of Local (NCIT Members) 20 ,Maximum of remain part of the Srilanka  Companies 20 . This time Srilankan & Indian Companies will get special Offers/Waives  in Booth fees and facilities.
