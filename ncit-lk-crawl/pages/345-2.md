Digital Transformation Workshop and Exhibition that was held on 26th October 2017 in collaboration with World University Service of Canada (WUSC), ICT Agency of Sri Lanka (ICTA), and Eastern District ICT forums namely Trincomalee Chamber of ICT (TCICT), Computer & Visual Technology Technicians’ Association Batticaloa (CVTTAB) and ICT Development Association of Ampara District. NCIT Participated in the event with its members. A panel discussion led by Mr. T. Thavaruban, Chairman NCIT were very enlightening and made everyone to understand the usage of Technology in the day-to-day life. Mr.Sharanyan sharma delivered Keynote talk on Digital Marketting,E commerce  and Entrepreneurship

NCIT made arrangement to members put Stall in the Exhibition for free of charge and arranged accomodation and traveling facilities with the support of WUSC.
