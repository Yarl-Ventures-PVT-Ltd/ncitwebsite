NCIT Talk session was held on  29.10.2018 at NCIT Business Incubation Center  Scientist Dr.Narayanan Kannan was delivered Talk in the title of “Criteria of global Citizenry”. He spent 2.30 hrs with youths and Professionals .It was great knowledge Sharing was through his experience in India ,Japan ,Germany, Malaysia,Korea ,Srilanka

Dr.Narayanan Kannan is A professional environmental scientist and  German national working as Adjunct Professor at AIMST University,  malasiya; with over quarter century of international research experience in various aspects of Persistent Organic Pollutants (POPs) including the fate and transport, risk-assessment, degradation processes, human exposure pathways, biogeochemistry. Worked in Japan, Germany, Korea and India.
