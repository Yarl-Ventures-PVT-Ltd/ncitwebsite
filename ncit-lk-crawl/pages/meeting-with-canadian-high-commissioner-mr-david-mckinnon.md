NCIT had meeting with Canadian High Commissioner Mr.David McKinnon and his team today 30.11.2017 at Valamuri hotel Jaffna.Meeting was  arranged by WUSC. After take charge as HC in Srilanka ,Mr.David McKinnon visited to jaffna first time today and interested to know the activities  lead by  Chambers and forums where WUSC supported , He met all  key officials of ICT , Constructions , Automobile,Tourism Associations along with   WUSC regional team

On behalf of NCIT,  Chairman T.Thavaruban and Vice chairman Y.Sharmmhik were participated in the discussion and explained all about NCIT , its past activities   and needs of ICT industry sectors here in Northern province.

Finally on behalf of NCIT, chairman Thavaruban gave memorandum to David McKinnon
