NCIT organized Northern IT SME Business to Business Expo 2017 and ICT awareness program for other industry businesses in the northern province. It was held in Jaffna Valampuri Hotel(Known as Green Grass Hotel) on 31st may 2017 9AM to 4PM .

![](http://www.ncit.lk/wp-content/uploads/2017/06/18922650_2315110638715116_705826535335223449_o.jpg)

NCIT organized it in collaboration with **CCIY** and ICT Agency ( [**ICTA**](http://www.icta.lk/)) of Sri Lanka is the National partner of this national initiative. This programme is in-line with ICTA’s overall vision of creating a Digitally Inclusive Sri Lanka via empowering the citizens digitally.

**Dr.S.Shivany** from department of marketing ,University of Jaffna  delivered keynote speech on “e-Commerce Transformation of SME and Digital Marketing Strategies”. **Mr.K.Sarveswaran** from Department of Computer Science ,University   of  Jaffna delivered keynote speech on “Digital Transformation”

Selected IT SMEs from northern province were exhibited their Products and solutions. Hundred of SME stake holders were participated in the event
