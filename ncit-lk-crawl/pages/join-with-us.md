To Get Membership

**Step 1:** Download Membership [Form Here](http://www.ncit.lk/wp-content/uploads/2017/03/NCIT_Membership_Form.pdf)  or Collect from Office.

**Step 2:** email to support@ncit.lk /Handover  Filled form with copy of Business Registration Certificate \[If registered Company\], If individual Send with NIC Photo Copy.

**Step 3:** NCIT will inform about your membership approval via Post or Email

**Step 4:** You need to Pay the Membership fee which will be given in the approval letter.

**Step 5:** NCIT  will Display the members in Website
