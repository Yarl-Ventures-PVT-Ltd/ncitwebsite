Fallowing Key decisions have been taken in July 2016 Board Meeting which held on 19th July 2016 in jaffna

1. Conduct at least 3 local Awareness community events in every month.
2. Establish Co-working Place for NCIT members before 2017 June
3. Monthly Business Forum in a suitable common Place from August 2016
4. Investor Forum meeting in Jaffna to boost Startup Weekend Jaffna Teams and New Startups.
