NCIT joined as an Official challenge partner of  the   Climathon Jaffna 18-hour hackathon which goint to take place in Jaffna City on  24 & 25 October 2019. NCIT signed MOU with city enabler for this Climathon.

[Climathon](https://www.facebook.com/hashtag/climathon?source=feed_text&epa=HASHTAG) [#Jaffna](https://www.facebook.com/hashtag/jaffna?source=feed_text&epa=HASHTAG) is a 18-hour hackathon in which people from all over Sri Lanka will have the chance to team up with like-minded professionals with the end goal of developing sustainable solutions for the provided challenges of Jaffna City.

Join the Climathon in Jaffna to connect with city officials and partners to bring about a shared vision for a sustainable city. Turn this vision into a reality by working together with academics, entrepreneurs, or students to find innovative solutions and win support. Come with an existing idea and an inspiring team. Experts and mentors will support your team during 18 hours in finding the best solutions to the provided challenges of Jaffna City. **[Join Here](https://climathon.climate-kic.org/en/participate?organisation_id=412)**

Northern Chamber of Information Technology NCIT will organize an ideation workshop for its members to develop solutions for the pre-defined climate challenges of Climathon Jaffna. Northern Chamber of Information Technology NCIT will support the data collection and further development of ideas brought by its members for Climathon Jaffna.
