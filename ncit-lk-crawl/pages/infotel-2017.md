The Federation of Information Technology Industry Sri Lanka (FITIS) had their flagship Infotel exhibition for the 25th successive year with many novel and innovative features at BMICH from Nov 2 – 5.

![](http://www.ncit.lk/wp-content/uploads/2017/11/IMG_20171105_173038.jpg)

Northern Chamber of Information Technology (NCIT) taking part in the exhibition for the first time, powered by FITIS and World University Service of Canada (WUSC) and in collaboration with Vavuniya Information & Communication Association (ViCTA) and Mannar Federation of Information & Communication Technology (ManFICT).
