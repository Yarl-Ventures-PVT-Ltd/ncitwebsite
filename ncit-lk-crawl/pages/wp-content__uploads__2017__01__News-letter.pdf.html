Introduction

Northern Chamber of Information Technology (NCIT) is the gateway to the information technology market and industry in North Part of Sri Lanka. NCIT was established in 22nd of February 2016 in order to utilize the synergy of all the different ICT associations in Northern Province under one roof that is needed to take the ICT industry forward.

The NCIT covers Jaffna, Kilinochchi, Mannar, Mullaithivu and Vavuniya districts under Northern Province of the Sri Lanka. More specifically the NCIT brings together ICT Associations, businesses, groups and individuals into a community where they can share ideas on how to promote and develop NCIT and ICT enabled Industries.

NCIT is dedicated to smoothing the ride of its members on Information highway with a passion to be the worldwide IT service provider of choice and committed to provide its members with the best return on investment using an excellent talent pool in multiple business domains and technologies. NCIT has now grown from under 20 members to over 40+ members.

Membership

The NCIT membership categorized in 6 types and Applicant’s main business Activity/Core Business should be in ICT within Northern Province. Membership period starts in April of every year and it should be renewed yearly. Membership fee can be decided by Chamber‘s Board time to time.

Membership types:

1. Full Membership:

Full membership only for Registered/Approved IT organizations of Information and Communication Technology sector in Northern Province of Sri Lanka which has head office in North Province.

2. Offshore Membership:

Offshore membership only for offshore companies who have branch office in Northern Province.

3. Association Membership:

Association membership only for non-profit organizations from Northern Province.

4. Professional Individual Membership:

Professional Individual Membership for individuals IT experts/Academics/Consultants

5. Freelancer Membership:

Freelancer membership only for freelancers and un-registered, virtual companies.

6. Student Membership:

Student membership for who are studying and completed 18 years old

Board

The board member of NCIT should have full membership and no any proxy allowed for board meetings/ representation. Board will Contains 9 members from each sectors (Technology & development 3 members, Hardware and networking 2 members, Education and training 2 members, Service, innovation and general forum 2 members). The board members will select Chairman and Vice chairman from Board and will Appoint sub committees according to the needs.
