[Notice](https://www.ncit.lk/category/notice/), [Whats New](https://www.ncit.lk/category/whatsnew/) / [webadmin](https://www.ncit.lk/author/webadmin/ "View all posts by webadmin")

Northern Chamber of Information Technology (NCIT) scheduled ICTA’s “Northern Go Digital 2019” Event in “April 6th  2019 9AM to 4PM ” in Jaffna with the collaboration with CCIY and other Regional IT forums. We will allocate Stall for selected maximum 15-20 IT companies who located in North part of Srilanka with the guidance of ICTA.
