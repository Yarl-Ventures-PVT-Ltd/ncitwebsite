The Northern Chamber of Information Technology (NCIT) is delighted to announce the successful conclusion of its Annual General Meeting (AGM), held on Saturday, 18 July 2026.

We are pleased to share that all Board Members were elected unanimously, reflecting the trust, unity, and shared commitment of our members towards advancing the ICT industry in Northern Sri Lanka.

This marks the beginning of an exciting new chapter for NCIT. As the unified voice of the Northern ICT industry, the newly elected Board is committed to fostering innovation, strengthening industry collaboration, empowering startups and entrepreneurs, supporting talent development, building strategic partnerships, and creating greater opportunities for technology businesses to thrive both nationally and globally.

Together, we look forward to building a stronger, more connected, and future-ready ICT ecosystem that drives sustainable economic growth and positions the Northern Province as a leading technology and innovation hub in Sri Lanka.

We extend our sincere appreciation to our members for their continued confidence and support, and congratulate all the newly elected Board Members. We wish them every success as they lead NCIT through the 2026–2027 term.

Together, let’s shape the future of Northern ICT.
